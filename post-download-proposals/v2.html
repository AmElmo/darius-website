A note while you wait

Thank you for trying Darius.

Right now, somewhere on your Mac, a small .dmg file is finishing its journey. In about a minute, you'll drag an icon to your Applications folder, and Darius will be yours.

We built Darius for a specific feeling — the one where you want an AI to actually do something, not just talk about it. On your machine. With your files. In parallel with your own work, instead of fighting it for attention.

That's why it's native macOS, not a tab. Why it runs many agents at once, not one at a time. And why it works with any model — your Claude key, your OpenAI key, or a local MLX model that never leaves your laptop.

Try this first
Read everything in my Downloads folder and tell me what's worth keeping.

It's a small, honest test. Darius will look. It will think. It will come back with a real answer, organized the way you would have organized it yourself if you had the afternoon. From there, you'll know what to ask next.

One last thing: this is a beta. Some edges are sharp. If you find one — or just want to tell us something — write to hi@darius.app. A human reads every note.

Welcome aboard.
